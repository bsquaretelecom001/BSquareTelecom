Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how B Square Telecom handles payment issues, internet access problems and refund requests.

1. Successful Payment but No Access Code

If your payment is successful but you do not receive your internet access code, contact us with your payment reference and phone number. We will investigate and resolve the issue.

2. Duplicate Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us with the relevant transaction references. Verified duplicate payments will be reviewed for correction or refund.

3. Activated Internet Plans

Once an access code has been issued and used, or the purchased internet service has been consumed, the purchase is generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or approved after investigation.

4. Service Interruptions

If a network interruption prevents you from using a purchased plan, contact us. We will review the issue and determine an appropriate resolution.

5. How to Request a Refund

Contact us through our Contact Page or WhatsApp at +234 812 130 2528. Include your payment reference and phone number.

6. Approved Refunds

Approved refunds will normally be processed through the original payment method where possible. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.
