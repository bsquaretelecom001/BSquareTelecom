Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, selected internet plan and payment reference when you purchase a service or contact us.

We use your information to process orders, confirm payments, provide internet access, troubleshoot problems and respond to customer enquiries.

Information may be shared with payment processors, website hosting providers and service providers where necessary to deliver our services or comply with the law.

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request correction of inaccurate personal information or ask questions about information you have provided.
